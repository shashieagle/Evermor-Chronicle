# PROJECT CONTEXT

We are building the About page for the Evermor website.

Evermor is not a wedding photography company.

It is an editorial storytelling studio that preserves the beginning of a family's story.

The Homepage, Beginnings page and Individual Beginning pages are already complete.

The About page must inherit EXACTLY the same visual language.

DO NOT redesign the website.

Reuse:

• Existing navigation
• Typography
• Colour palette
• Beige background
• Grid system
• Whitespace
• Components
• Animation utilities
• Spacing scale

The About page should feel like another chapter in the same book.

------------------------------------------------------------

# OBJECTIVE

This page has ONE purpose.

Help visitors trust the people behind Evermor.

The Homepage already explains the philosophy.

The Beginnings page already demonstrates the work.

This page should simply introduce the people behind Evermor in a warm, honest and understated way.

Do NOT repeat ideas already explained elsewhere.

Do NOT make this page feel like an agency profile.

Less is more.

------------------------------------------------------------

# PAGE STRUCTURE

The page consists of only THREE movements.

1. Hero

2. Editorial Story

3. Quiet Invitation

Nothing else.

No timelines.

No process diagrams.

No service explanation.

No philosophy cards.

No statistics.

No awards.

No client logos.

No counters.

------------------------------------------------------------

# HERO

Height:
Approximately 85vh.

------------------------------------------------------------

# HERO IMAGE

One calm documentary-style portrait of Shashikanth and Deepika.

Preferred imagery:

• Walking together

• Sitting naturally

• Looking through albums

• Talking together

• Quiet candid interaction

Avoid:

• Cameras

• Studio portraits

• Looking directly into the camera

• Dramatic wedding poses

The image should communicate warmth, trust and authenticity.

------------------------------------------------------------

# HERO LAYOUT

Minimal.

Large whitespace.

Editorial proportions.

Image should dominate.

Text should never compete with the image.

Desktop:

The image occupies most of the hero.

The copy is positioned comfortably within the composition.

Mobile:

Maintain hierarchy.

Do not simply scale down.

Ensure the image still feels immersive.

------------------------------------------------------------

# HERO TYPOGRAPHY

Headline

We are Shashikanth & Deepika.

Supporting text

Partners in life.

Partners in storytelling.

No buttons.

No CTA.

No icons.

No social media.

No decorative graphics.

------------------------------------------------------------

# TRANSITION

As the visitor scrolls...

The hero naturally gives way to the editorial story.

No divider.

No chapter heading.

No visual interruption.

Only generous whitespace.

------------------------------------------------------------

# EDITORIAL STORY

Maximum content width:

720–760px.

Centre aligned container.

Readable line length.

Large line spacing.

Comfortable paragraph spacing.

The page should feel like reading an editorial essay.

Use the following copy.

------------------------------------------------------------

We didn't begin this journey because we wanted to become wedding photographers.

We began because we were fascinated by people.

The relationships they build.

The families they belong to.

The stories they carry.

When we got married ourselves, something changed.

We realised the memories we treasured most weren't the perfectly posed photographs.

They were the moments we almost forgot.

A quiet glance.

Parents trying to hide their emotions.

Friends laughing between ceremonies.

Small conversations that no one planned.

That experience changed the way we approached every wedding that followed.

Today, we don't arrive with the intention of simply documenting a celebration.

We arrive with the responsibility of preserving the beginning of a family's story.

For us, this has never been just about photography.

It has always been about people.

------------------------------------------------------------

# TYPOGRAPHY

Never display this as one large block.

Break naturally.

Allow important thoughts to stand alone.

Whitespace creates rhythm.

Do not use bold text for emphasis.

Do not use decorative quotes.

------------------------------------------------------------

# QUIET INVITATION

After the editorial story...

Create a generous pause.

Approximately 180–220px vertical whitespace.

Then place one final editorial statement.

------------------------------------------------------------

If our story resonates with you,

perhaps it's time to begin yours.

------------------------------------------------------------

Below that, with generous spacing,

display a simple text link.

Begin Your Story →

Do NOT use a filled button.

Do NOT use an outlined button.

The interaction should feel like turning the page of a beautifully printed book.

On hover:

• subtle underline OR
• gentle opacity transition

Nothing more.

------------------------------------------------------------

# VISUAL LANGUAGE

Maintain the same editorial identity as the Homepage.

Minimal.

Calm.

Elegant.

Timeless.

No visual clutter.

No cards.

No borders.

No drop shadows.

No gradients.

No glassmorphism.

No unnecessary icons.

No decorative illustrations.

Whitespace is a design element.

------------------------------------------------------------

# ANIMATION

Reuse existing animation system.

Image fades in.

Text fades upward subtly.

Duration:

600–800ms.

No parallax.

No cinematic effects.

No scrolling tricks.

------------------------------------------------------------

# RESPONSIVENESS

Desktop:

Editorial composition.

Tablet:

Maintain proportions.

Mobile:

Reading should remain effortless.

Preserve generous spacing.

Do not compress the experience.

------------------------------------------------------------

# ACCESSIBILITY

Semantic HTML.

Proper heading hierarchy.

Accessible contrast.

Responsive typography.

Keyboard accessible navigation.

------------------------------------------------------------

# PERFORMANCE

Reuse existing components.

Reuse typography tokens.

Reuse spacing utilities.

Reuse animation utilities.

Do not duplicate styles.

Do not introduce new libraries.

------------------------------------------------------------

# FINAL EXPERIENCE

When someone finishes this page they should not feel like they have read an About Us page.

They should feel like they have met two people they would trust with preserving one of the most important days of their lives.

The final emotion should be calm, personal and reassuring.

The transition into "Begin Your Story" should feel inevitable rather than promotional.

Return clean, production-ready code consistent with the existing Evermor architecture.
